# Referrals and warm contacts (optional module)

Cold applications convert poorly; one warm contact often beats twenty cold
applies. This module is **off by default**, because outreach costs energy
and some people would rather not. Its state lives in `criteria.md` under
"Networking": `off`, `ask` (ask one question per core application), or
`active` (also find and draft).

During setup, explain the trade-off in one or two sentences and let them
pick. If they pick `off`, never bring it up again unless they do, or unless
calibration (see `tracker.md`) shows cold applications aren't converting;
then mention it once as an option.

## `ask` mode (light)

For each **Full or Medium tier** application only (decided in `tailor.md`
Step 4), before the resume plan, ask one question:

> Do you know anyone at <Company>, or anyone who used to work there?

- **No** → move on. Don't ask again for this application.
- **Yes** → note the contact on the tracker record and draft a short
  message (below). Suggested order: the contact submits the referral
  internally first, then they apply; if no reply in about three business
  days, apply anyway.

## `active` mode

Everything in `ask`, plus, with a browser tool available:

1. Look for 1 or 2 plausible people at the company: alumni of their school,
   people from a past employer, someone in the same function one or two
   levels up. Use public profile pages and search only, at the pace a
   person browsing would; don't scrape at volume. Automated browsing of
   LinkedIn is against its User Agreement; the same caution as the daily
   screen's browser run applies.
2. List them with why each is a reasonable ask. The user picks; Claude
   never contacts anyone on its own.
3. Draft the message for the picked person.

Keep a weekly target only if they want one (record it in `criteria.md`).

## Drafting the message

- Short: 3 to 5 sentences. A specific connection first (same school, same
  past company, their post on X), one line on what the user does, the exact
  role with link, one clear ask ("would you be open to referring me, or a
  15-minute chat about the team?").
- No flattery paragraphs, no attaching the full resume in the first
  message unless the platform makes it natural.
- Match the user's own tone. If they have a writing-style preference saved,
  use it.
- Put it in the platform's input box or a draft, or just in chat. **Never
  send it.** They review and send.

Record on the tracker: contact name, how they know them, date messaged,
reply, whether a referral was submitted. Referrals are a column in the
calibration, so it becomes visible whether they work for this person.
