从看 JD 到面完复盘From the posting to the debrief

按一次求职真实发生的顺序排的。In the order a job search actually happens.

1

建你的素材库Build your evidence library

读你的简历，问几个具体问题，把每段经历的真实数字和多种写法存下来。之后所有简历都从这里取。It reads your resume, asks concrete questions, and stores every real number and several framings of each role. Every resume is built from this.

2

值不值得投Is it worth applying?

两个分：能不能做、值不值得。每条要求对上你的经历，缺口直接说，并告诉你上什么课、做什么项目能补上。Two scores: can you do it, is it worth it. Every requirement matched to your experience, gaps named with a course or project that would close them, and your edge.

3

一页纸简历A one-page resume

先给你看方案，你点头再出 Word 和 PDF。自动检查溢页、AI 腔、页面填满没有。You approve a plan first, then get Word and PDF. Checked automatically for overflow, filler words and page fill.

4

申请表答案库Form answer bank

同类题攒起来，下次改一句就能用。逼你往高了答的题，先给你诚实的选项。Recurring questions saved for reuse. Questions that push you to overstate get the honest option first.

5

投递记录和复盘Tracker and check-ins

连 JD 全文一起存。投满 20 份，按岗位类型和投入程度算回音率，告诉你该往哪调。Saved with the full job description. At 20 applications, reply rates by role type and effort, and what to change.

6

面试准备Interview prep

预测题和用你真实经历写的答案，薪资怎么说。面完陪你复盘，哪里卡了、下次怎么说。Likely questions, answers from your real experience, how to talk salary. Afterwards, where you got stuck and what to say next time.

它跟别的简历工具不一样的地方What makes it different

不帮你编It won't make things up

素材库里没有的，它会问你"做过没有"。没做过就标成缺口，头衔照原样写，估出来的数字要你点头才用。If it's not in your library, it asks. If you haven't done it, it's a gap. Titles stay as given, and estimates need your OK.

会叫你别投It will tell you to skip

最近一段经历对不上、硬性要求有缺口、公司口碑有红旗，它会直接说，省下的时间留给值得的岗位。Recent experience doesn't match, a must-have is missing, the company has red flags: it says so, and you spend the time where it counts.

用你的数据调方向It learns from your replies

不只是记录投了什么。它看哪类岗位真的有回音，告诉你下一步把力气花在哪。It doesn't just log what you sent. It looks at which kinds of roles actually reply and tells you where to put your effort next.

不用 Claude？Not using Claude?

整套流程也有一份通用的提示词版，贴进 ChatGPT、Gemini、DeepSeek、Kimi 的自定义指令就能用，再配一个一页纸简历模板。少的只是自动出文件和浏览器自动搜。The whole workflow also comes as one portable prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or Kimi, plus a one-page resume template. You only lose the auto-built file and the browser search.

关于 LinkedIn 自动搜索：About the LinkedIn browser mode: 可选的浏览器模式会在你的 LinkedIn 账号上自动搜索和收藏岗位，这违反 LinkedIn 用户协议，账号可能被限制。默认关闭，开不开、风险由你自己决定。默认的贴链接模式没有这个问题。The optional browser mode searches and saves jobs on your LinkedIn account. That's against LinkedIn's User Agreement and accounts can be restricted. It's off by default; turning it on is your call and your risk. The default paste-links mode has no such issue.

评分和建议是 AI 根据公开信息和你自己的资料给的，可能出错，不构成职业建议。投出去的每份材料请自己过目。Scores and advice are an AI's reading of public information and your own notes. They can be wrong and aren't career advice. Check everything before you send it.
