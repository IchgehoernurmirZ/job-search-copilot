从看 JD 到面完复盘From the posting to the debrief

按找工真实发生的顺序排（兵荒马乱也算）。In the order a job search actually happens (chaos included).

1

建你的素材库Build your evidence library

读你的简历，问一些有点烦但很有用的问题，把每段经历的真实数字和几种说法都存下来。之后每份简历都从这里取材。It reads your resume, asks you the annoying-but-useful questions, and saves every real number plus a few ways to say each thing. Every resume pulls from here.

2

值不值得投Is it worth applying?

两个分：你能不能做、值不值得投。每条要求对上你的经历，缺口直说（还告诉你上什么课、做什么项目能补上），再挖出你的隐藏优势。Two scores: can you do it, and is it worth it. Every requirement matched to your experience, gaps called out (with a course or project to fix them), plus your secret edge.

3

一页纸简历A one-page resume

先给你看方案，你点头才出 Word 和 PDF。自动检查有没有溢到第二页、有没有 AI 腔、页面有没有空一大块。You OK the plan first, then you get Word + PDF. Auto-checked for spilling onto page 2, AI-speak filler, and awkward empty space.

4

申请表答案库Form answer bank

那些每张表都要填一遍的题？攒起来，下次改一句就能用。遇到诱导你往高了答的题，先给你诚实版本。Those questions you answer on every single form? Saved, so next time it's a one-line tweak. And when a question nudges you to oversell, you get the honest answer first.

5

投递记录和复盘Tracker and check-ins

连 JD 全文一起存（岗位说没就没）。投满 20 份，帮你看哪类岗位有回音、该怎么调。Saves the full job description (because postings vanish). At 20 applications it shows what's getting replies and what to change.

6

面试准备Interview prep

预测题、用你真实经历写的答案、薪资怎么谈。面完陪你复盘：哪里卡壳了、下次怎么说。Likely questions, answers from your real experience, how to talk money. After, a debrief: where you froze and what to say next time.

它跟别的简历工具哪里不一样Why it's not just another resume tool

不帮你编It won't make stuff up

素材库里没有的，它会问你"做过没？"。没做过就是缺口，不会硬塞进简历。头衔照原样写，估出来的数字要你点头才用。Not in your library? It asks. Never done it? It's a gap, not a bullet point. Titles stay as given, and estimates need your OK.

会劝你别投It'll tell you to skip

最近经历对不上、硬性要求缺一块、公司风评一堆红旗，它都会直说。精力留给值得的岗位。Wrong recent experience, a missing must-have, sketchy reviews: it'll say so, so your energy goes to the jobs that deserve it.

用你的数据调方向It learns from your replies

不只是记你投了啥。它会看哪类岗位真的回你，告诉你下一步往哪使劲。靠数据，不靠玄学。It doesn't just log what you sent. It checks which roles actually write back and tells you where to aim next. Data over vibes.

不用 Claude？没关系Not on Claude? No worries

整套流程还有一份通用提示词，贴进 ChatGPT、Gemini、DeepSeek、Kimi 就能用，外加一个一页纸简历模板。只是少了自动出文件和浏览器自动搜。The whole workflow also comes as one copy-paste prompt for ChatGPT, Gemini, DeepSeek or Kimi, plus a one-page resume template. You just miss the auto-built files and the browser search.

通用提示词Portable prompt ↗ 简历模板Resume template ↗

关于 LinkedIn 自动搜索，先说清楚：Heads up about LinkedIn: 可选的浏览器模式会用你自己的 LinkedIn 账号自动搜、自动收藏岗位，这违反 LinkedIn 用户协议，账号可能被限制。默认是关的，开不开、风险都由你自己决定。默认的贴链接模式完全没这个问题。The optional browser mode searches and saves jobs on your own LinkedIn account. That's against LinkedIn's User Agreement and accounts can get restricted. It's off by default, so turning it on is your call and your risk. The default paste-the-links mode is totally fine.

评分和建议是 AI 根据公开信息和你自己的资料给的，会出错，也不算职业建议。投出去之前，每份材料都请自己过一眼！Scores and advice are an AI reading public info and your own notes. It can be wrong and it's not career advice, so please read everything before you hit send!
